## Full description
NomNap is a calm, beautifully simple way to track your baby's day.

Four actions, two taps: start sleep, end sleep, start feed, end feed. NomNap does the
timing math for you and shows a live timer while a session is running.

• Track — big Sleep and Feed cards. One tap to start, one tap to stop. See today's totals
  and recent activity at a glance.
• Stats — daily averages, session averages, longest sleep, and a per-day breakdown.
• History — every event grouped by day. Tap to edit a time or delete an entry.
• Diapers — log wet/dirty changes alongside sleep and feeds.
• Share — invite your partner or a caregiver with a code and keep the same log in sync
  across your devices.
• Backups — export your data to a file and import it on a new device, anytime.

Private by design: no sign-up and no personal account — NomNap signs you in anonymously,
so we never ask for your name, email, or phone number. Your log syncs securely so you can
use it across devices and share it with a caregiver, and it works offline too. No ads, no
tracking.

Made for tired parents who just want to tap and go.

## Privacy
NomNap uses anonymous sign-in (no personal account) and stores your log in Google Firebase
(Cloud Firestore, EU region) so it can sync across devices and be shared with caregivers.
No ads, no cross-app tracking, no data selling. Answer Apple's privacy questionnaire as
follows (all "Not linked to you", "Not used for tracking"):

- **Health & Fitness** → *Other Health/Fitness data*: sleep, feed, diaper, and growth
  entries you log. Purpose: App Functionality.
- **Diagnostics** → *Crash Data* (via Firebase Crashlytics). Purpose: App Functionality.
- **Contact Info** → *Email Address*: ONLY collected if a user optionally includes it when
  sending in-app feedback; can be left blank. Purpose: App Functionality (to reply).
- **Identifiers**: the anonymous Firebase user ID / Crashlytics installation ID. Purpose:
  App Functionality. Not used to track across apps or websites.
- Baby name is optional and user-entered; treat as part of the Health/Fitness log.
- Privacy policy URL: https://emilgras.github.io/nomnap/privacy.html
