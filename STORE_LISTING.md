## Full description
NomNap is a calm, beautifully simple way to track your baby's day.

Four actions, two taps: start sleep, end sleep, start feed, end feed. NomNap does the
timing math for you and shows a live timer while a session is running.

• Track — big Sleep and Feed cards. One tap to start, one tap to stop. See today's totals
  and recent activity at a glance.
• Stats — daily averages, session averages, longest sleep, and a per-day breakdown.
• History — every event grouped by day. Tap to edit a time or delete an entry.
• Diapers — log wet/dirty changes alongside sleep and feeds.
• Backups — export your data to a file and import it on a new device, anytime.

Private by design: everything stays on your device. No account, no sign-up, no ads, no
analytics, no servers. Works fully offline.

Made for tired parents who just want to tap and go.
