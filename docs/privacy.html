NomNap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

NomNap is a baby sleep, feed, and diaper tracker for parents and caregivers. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to make the app work, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising. This policy explains what we store, where, and why.

No sign-up, no personal account

You don't create an account or give us your name, email, or phone number to use NomNap. On first launch the app signs in anonymously through Firebase Authentication, which issues a random, anonymous identifier for your device. That identifier is not linked to your real-world identity.

What we store, and where

To let you keep your log in sync and optionally share it with another caregiver, the activity you record is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), hosted in the European Union. Google acts as our data processor. The data is associated only with the anonymous identifier described above.

We store:

  • Activity you log — sleep sessions, feeds, diaper changes, and growth measurements (such as weight and length), each with a timestamp.
  • An optional baby name, only if you choose to enter one. You can leave it blank.
  • Household & sharing data — a random household ID, the anonymous IDs of caregivers who have joined it, and any invite codes you generate to share access.

We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or precise device identifiers as part of normal use.

Offline use & local cache

NomNap works offline. Your data is cached on your device so the app keeps working without a connection, and changes sync to Firestore automatically when you're back online.

Sharing with caregivers

If you create an invite code and share it with another person, redeeming that code lets their device join your household and see and edit the same log. Only people you share a code with can access your data. You control who you invite.

Crash and diagnostic data

To find and fix bugs, NomNap uses Firebase Crashlytics, which collects crash reports and basic diagnostic information (such as the type of crash, device model, and operating system version) when the app crashes. This data is not used to identify you and is not used for advertising or tracking. Crash reporting is disabled in development builds.

Optional feedback

If you send feedback from within the app, we store the message you write. If you also choose to include your email address so we can reply, we store that email for that purpose only. Leaving the email blank is fine — feedback can be sent anonymously.

Backups & export

NomNap lets you export your data to a .json backup file and import it again. When you export, your device's standard share sheet opens and you decide where the file goes (Files, iCloud/Drive, email, etc.). We never receive a copy of exported files.

Data retention & deletion

Your data stays in Firestore so it's available across your devices and to caregivers you've invited. You can delete individual entries in the app at any time. To have your household's data deleted entirely, email us at the address below and we'll remove it.

Tracking & advertising

None. NomNap contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics for marketing, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children's privacy

NomNap is intended for use by parents and caregivers, not by children. The information you enter is about your child and is provided by you as the parent or guardian. We do not knowingly allow children to create or use the app themselves.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
