NomNap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 June 2026

NomNap is a baby sleep & feed tracker. We designed it to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

None. NomNap does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. We have no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Where your data lives

Everything you record in NomNap — sleep sessions, feeds, diaper changes — is stored locally on your device only. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

Backups & export

NomNap lets you export your data to a .json backup file and import it again. When you tap export, your device's standard share sheet opens and you decide where the file goes (Files, iCloud/Drive, email, etc.). NomNap has no visibility into or control over that destination, and we never receive a copy.

Permissions

NomNap only accesses files you explicitly pick (for import) or save (for export) through the system file picker and share sheet. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, photos, or microphone.

Children's privacy

NomNap is a tool for parents and caregivers. It does not knowingly collect any information from children, and indeed collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
