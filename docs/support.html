Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. NomNap signs you in anonymously — there's no name, email, or password to set up. Just open the app and start tracking.

How do I share tracking with my partner or a caregiver?

In the app, open the caregivers screen and create an invite code. Share that code with the other person; when they enter it on their device, you'll both see and update the same log, kept in sync.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can keep logging without a connection, and your changes sync automatically once you're back online.

How do I back up or move my data to a new device?

Use the export option to save a backup file, then import it on another device. Sharing an invite code also brings your log to a new device.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual entries in the app at any time. To have all of your household's data removed, email emil.gras@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.
